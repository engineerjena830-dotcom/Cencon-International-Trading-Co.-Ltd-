Frequently Asked Questions

What services does CIT × USIRI offer?

CIT supports product sourcing, supplier payment assistance, and air cargo coordination in China. USIRI handles transportation from Dar es Salaam to your agreed destination in Tanzania. Availability and terms are confirmed with you for each enquiry.

Can you help me find products in China?

Yes, CIT helps customers identify products and suitable suppliers in China. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp, and the team will discuss it with you. Sourcing outcomes cannot be guaranteed in advance.

Can you assist with payments to suppliers in China?

CIT assists with supplier payments in China, subject to agreed arrangements. The applicable terms and charges are confirmed individually before anything proceeds.

Do you coordinate air cargo shipping to Tanzania?

Yes, CIT helps coordinate air cargo shipping from China to Tanzania. Share your cargo details, and the team will discuss your requirements, shipment details, charges, and timing with you directly.

Can my cargo be delivered outside Dar es Salaam?

USIRI handles transportation from Dar es Salaam to your agreed destination in Tanzania. Whether a specific destination can be served is confirmed with USIRI when you enquire.

How do I receive a quotation?

Message CIT or USIRI on WhatsApp, or send the enquiry form below, which opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. Describe your product or cargo and destination, and the team will confirm your quotation directly.

Are all services included in one package?

No. Services are arranged according to what you need, so you can enquire about one service or several. What is included, plus charges and timing, is confirmed individually.
